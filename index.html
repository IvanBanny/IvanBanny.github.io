Dirtflow

2026 - present / Independent research

Flow matching for 3D generation and editing.

I independently develop Dirtflow, adapting pretrained TRELLIS models with two-stage flow matching to generate geometry and full Minecraft block states from text and 3D context.

I built the data pipeline for nearly 470,000 builds and the distributed training and evaluation systems. My experiments study learning dynamics and how geometry constrains material generation.

Droomba

Ongoing research / Serpentine, TU/e

World models for autonomous flight.

I'm working on model-based reinforcement learning for drone navigation in unfamiliar environments. The approach learns action-conditioned world models and trains control policies through imagined trajectories.

I'm studying how perception and memory affect generalization to unseen layouts, with transfer from simulation to physical flight as a central problem. The goal is real-time onboard control without a prebuilt map or prescribed route, within the drone's compute and latency limits.
